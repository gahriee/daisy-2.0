## Prompt 3: Fix Paper Detector Accuracy on Warm Wooden Desks
**Timestamp:** 2026-11-23 17:33:32 (UTC+8)

### Prompt Goals
* Fix inaccurate bottom-left and bottom-right corner detection when scanning a paper document lying on a bright warm wooden desk (where raw `jscanify.findPaperContour` grayscale Canny merged the paper with the wooden desk and snapped the bottom corners to the outer edges of the camera frame).

### Key Decisions & Rationale
* **Root Cause from User Screenshot Analysis:** Sampled RGB channels across the user's screenshot (`472x1024`) and found that the warm wooden desk on the left and right had nearly the same grayscale luminance (`~130–155`) as the shadowed paper (`~130–160`), causing raw grayscale `cv.Canny(img, imgGray, 50, 200)` inside `jscanify.findPaperContour` to miss the paper-to-wood boundary and follow the outer desk edges to `(0, H)` and `(W, H)`. However, the paper is achromatic (`G ≈ B`, `|G - B| <= 4`) whereas the wooden desk has strong yellow/warm chroma (`G - B = 35..61`, `R - B = 65..93`).
* **Chroma-Suppressed OpenCV + `jscanify` Segmentation (`detectByChromaSuppressedOpenCv` in `src/lib/document-detect.ts`):** Pre-computes a neutral-paper buffer (`b - 1.6 * max(0, g - b) - 0.35 * (max(r,g,b) - min(r,g,b))`) where wooden/warm surfaces drop to `0..15` while white/shadowed paper stays at `100..160`, runs OpenCV Gaussian blur + Otsu threshold + `cv.MORPH_CLOSE` + `cv.MORPH_OPEN` + external contour + `cv.convexHull`, and passes the hull to `jscanify`'s `scanner.getCornerPoints` (with `jscanify.findPaperContour` as fallback). Verified against the user's screenshot viewfinder (`402x536`), where detected corners moved from `(0, 535)` / `(401, 535)` directly onto the true bottom paper corners `(22, 472)` / `(368, 469)`.
