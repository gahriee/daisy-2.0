## Executive Summary
Simplified Daisy's owner experience for senior-citizen usability across two major changes: (1) consolidated 6 technical settings screens into one calm **Property & stay settings** page at `/settings` while moving billing/receipt policies, upload/offline limits, SMTP credentials, and Semaphore SMS credentials/template/frequency to validated server environment variables, and (2) removed the `RoomType` abstraction end-to-end (schema, migration, domain services, UI, sidebar, transients/public `/book`, reports/exports, offline vault, seed, docs, and tests) so rooms are created and priced directly one by one. Applied and verified migration `20261127201000_remove_room_types` on both `dev` and `production` (explicitly authorized by the Owner).

## Key Architectural & Technical Decisions
* **Centralized Server-Only Env Config (`src/config/business-config.ts`):** Added Zod-validated env resolution for billing policies (`BILLING_DAY_OF_MONTH`, `BILLING_GRACE_PERIOD_DAYS`, `BILLING_PRORATION_POLICY`, `BILLING_DEPOSIT_POLICY`, `BILLING_OVERPAYMENT_POLICY`, `INVOICE_PREFIX`, `RECEIPT_PREFIX`), operational limits (`OFFLINE_UNLOCK_MAX_DAYS`, `PROOF_MAX_MB`, `PROOF_MAX_FILES_PER_RECORD`, `DEFAULT_CHECK_IN_TIME`, `DEFAULT_CHECK_OUT_TIME`), Semaphore SMS (`SEMAPHORE_*`), and SMTP (`SMTP_*`, `MAIL_FROM`, `MAIL_FROM_NAME`) with Philippine boarding-house defaults and fail-fast validation. Left legacy `BusinessSettings` columns untouched in PostgreSQL for non-destructive rollback safety while dropping DB credential reads/writes and deleting `src/server/security/secret-box.ts`.
* **Single-Page Settings & 1-Tap Navigation:** Consolidated `/settings` into one `property` module form (Property details, Check-in/out times, and an Overdue rent texts On/Off toggle + send time), redirected legacy `/settings/{profile,policies,operations,sms,email}` routes to `/settings`, collapsed Sidebar `Settings`, `Rooms`, and `Transient rooms` into direct 1-tap links, removed blocking policy setup alerts, and replaced technical SMS phrasing in Notices with everyday language.
* **Data-Preserving `RoomType` Removal (`20261127201000_remove_room_types`):** Backfilled any null `Room.baseMonthlyRate` (Boarding) or `Room.transientDailyRate` (Transient) from `RoomType` before tightening `Room_building_rates_check` (requiring non-null `transientDailyRate >= 0` on `TRANSIENT` rooms), dropping `Room.roomTypeId`, and dropping `RoomType` and its indexes. Verified via `prisma migrate deploy`, `prisma migrate status`, and `pnpm db:check` on both `dev` and `production`.
* **Direct Room Pricing, Transients, Reports & Offline Vault:** Replaced Room Type selectors and prerequisite banners in `RoomForm`/`RoomsWorkspace` with direct required **Monthly rate (₱)** / **Daily rate (₱)** fields, redirected `/rooms/types` and `/transients/rooms/types`, removed `roomTypeName`/`roomTypeDailyRate` joins and public `/book` room-type filters/badges, removed `byRoomType` charts/tables/export breakdowns across Occupancy, Rent Roll, and Transient Revenue while keeping building, lease-expiry, and booking-status summaries, and updated `snapshot-selectors-extra.ts` to ignore legacy `room_type` projections while preserving `ChangeFeed`/`AuditLog` history.
* **Verification & Known Blocker:** Prettier, `pnpm typecheck`, targeted ESLint (0 errors, 0 warnings), `pnpm test:unit` (51 files, 219/219 passed), and `pnpm build` (85/85 routes) all passed. All 12 integration test files were updated to compile cleanly; live integration test execution against `.env.test.local` (`TEST_DATABASE_URL`) remains blocked by expired Neon `test-integration` branch credentials (never substituted with `dev` or `production`).
