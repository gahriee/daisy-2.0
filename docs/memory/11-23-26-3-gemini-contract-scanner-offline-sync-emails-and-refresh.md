## Key Architectural & Technical Decisions
* **Document Detection & Scan Pipeline (`src/lib/document-detect.ts`, `src/lib/document-scanner.ts`, `src/lib/opencv-loader.cjs`):** Adopted `jscanify` (`1.4.3`) + `@techstark/opencv-js` (`5.0.0-release.1`) with a CommonJS bridge, `canvas: false` in `pnpm-workspace.yaml`, and client Webpack Node built-in fallbacks. Added chroma-suppressed neutral-paper scoring (preventing warm wooden desks from merging with paper), open-first multi-threshold segmentation with solidity/border-corner filtering (resisting plaid fabric stripes), a 5-frame rolling-median `DocumentQuadStabilizer` (`searching` -> `stabilizing` -> `locked`) with WYSIWYG locked-corner capture, `ImageCapture.takePhoto()` full-sensor stills, and morphological background dilation + unsharp mask + steep ink stretch for crisp text.
* **Offline Sync Custody, Pending Outbox & PWA Precaching (`src/offline/*`, `src/sw.ts`, `public/sw.js`):** Wrapped `globalThis.fetch` in `defaultFetch` to fix browser `Illegal invocation` during bootstrap, added `resetOfflineVaultForNewPassphrase` and `listDecryptedPendingOutbox` for a safe **Forgot sync password?** flow and **Waiting to save online** UI, bumped `SW_CACHE_VERSION` to `daisy-shell-v4` to precache `/offline` hashed JS/CSS chunks on SW install, enabled PII-free `/offline` navigation fallback for online-only workspace routes, and synced `OfflineRouteFallback` to `window.location.pathname` via `useSyncExternalStore`.
* **Pending vs. Approved Booking Emails (`src/server/mail/booking-mailer.ts`, `src/features/transients/public-service.ts`, `src/app/api/public/booking-requests/route.ts`):** Added `sendBookingPendingEmailSafe` invoked concurrently with owner push outside the DB transaction for non-duplicate public submissions, differentiated pending (amber reference callout, live `/book#track` CTA, next steps) vs. approved (Check-in pass, room photo, Getting here + Grab buttons, no Track status button) layouts, normalized check-in/out times to 12-hour AM/PM, and added a Pending/Approved template selector to Settings test email previews.
* **Workspace Table Freshness, FileList Snapshot & Storefront Polish:** Added prop-to-state synchronization across `useOfflineSnapshotState` and 10 client workspace components, added online pathname-transition + bfcache `router.refresh()` in `WorkspaceShell`, added `revalidatePath` on `/api/sync/push` and device routes, snapshotted `Array.from(event.target.files ??
